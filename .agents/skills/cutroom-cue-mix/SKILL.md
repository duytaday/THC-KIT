Use Style → Sound effects/Music in the existing UI. Click belongs to actual
contact; confirmation belongs to visible success. Keep them separate even within
one shot. Expand a cue to change mute, gain, marker or signed frame offset.
Record a reason for intentional offsets. A trim moves sound and picture together.
Music Off and a deliberately silent cue are valid choices; a missing selected
asset is an actionable failure, never silent substitution.

Use original cleared local assets or the buyer’s cleared music. Keep voice, SFX
and music stems. Episode insertion must not mix artifact audio a second time or
restart the bed. Export through Render to invoke the same measurement hooks.
The Exports review offers matched-level voice/voice+SFX/full auditions; approval
always applies to the exact MP4. Measure approximately−16LUFS±2 and≤−1dBTP, then
have an audio-capable reviewer listen at1×. Numerical loudness cannot prove words
are intelligible. Without listening, retain NEEDS_REVIEW.
